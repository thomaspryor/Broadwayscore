About the Show

A programmer uncovers his algorithm’s role in a sweeping AI surveillance system.

Matthew Libby’s play examines the collision between innovation and oversight in the tech sector, following a young programmer who discovers that an algorithm he created underpins a far-reaching AI surveillance initiative. As he navigates the implications of his work, he confronts the structures, ambitions, and ethical compromises embedded within Silicon Valley. The play situates its narrative amid current debates about data collection and personal privacy, tracing the professional and personal consequences that emerge when he challenges the system built around his code.
