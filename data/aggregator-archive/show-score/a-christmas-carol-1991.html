About the Show

Production Covid-19 Safety Protocols

Jefferson Mays' solo rendition of Charles Dickens' classic Christmas story.

The classic tale of Ebenezer Scrooge and his visitation by spirits on the night before Christmas is brought to surprising new life as Tony Award® winner Jefferson Mays (I Am My Own Wife, Gentleman’s Guide…) embodies over 50 roles in a masterful display that must be seen to be believed.

Over the course of one evening, the greedy Scrooge comes to be taught the true meaning of Christmas, courtesy of the spirit of the deceased Jacob Marley and the Ghosts of Christmas Past, Present, and Future.

Tony Award® winner Michael Arden (Best Revival – Once on This Island) directs this eerie yet heartwarming story in a complete new light.
