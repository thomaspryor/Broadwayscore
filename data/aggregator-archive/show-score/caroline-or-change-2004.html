About the Show

Production Covid-19 Safety Protocols

Blues, gospel, motown, and Jewish folk music intertwine in this Tony-nominated musical.

Transferring from an award-winning run in the West End, Tony Kushner and Jeanine Tesori's musical "Caroline, or Change" stars Sharon D Clarke, who won an Olivier for Best Actress for her portrayal of Caroline. The story of an African-American maid in 1963 Louisiana, and set against the backdrop of the civil rights movement, the musical explores how, in times of great transformation, even the simplest acts can shake the earth.
