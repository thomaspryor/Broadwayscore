About the Show

A magical stage adaptation of Roald Dahl's popular children's book. Winner of 7 Olivier Awards.

Matilda Wormwood is a gifted young girl who realises she has certain magical powers. Raised by cruel parents, Matilda finds solace in library books where she creates her own vivid stories. At school, the mean Miss Trunchbull thinks that all children are maggots however Matilda finds companionship in her teacher Miss Honey.

Dennis Kelly's ('DNA', 'Utopia') adaptation of the beloved book by acclaimed children's author Roald Dahl. With music and lyrics by musician and comedian Tim Minchin ('Groundhog Day the Musical').

Winner of 99 international awards including the 2012 Olivier Award for Best New Musical. This production is recommended for ages 6+.
