About the Show

An Oxford scholar’s faith and reason are tested by love and loss with poet Joy Davidman.

Based on the true story of C.S. Lewis and Joy Davidman, Shadowlands follows the Oxford scholar and author of The Chronicles of Narnia as his intellectual world is transformed by an unexpected romance. When the spirited American poet Joy Davidman enters his life, their friendship deepens into a profound love that challenges Lewis’s faith and understanding of loss.

The play is written by William Nicholson, adapted from his own BAFTA Award-winning television script. Nicholson’s acclaimed work has also inspired a Tony Award-winning Broadway production and a major feature film directed by Richard Attenborough.

This revival is directed by Rachel Kavanaugh, whose previous credits include productions at the Chichester Festival Theatre and the West End.
