Design review · BRO-8203

Make the first moments count

Choose one desktop sign-in direction and one welcome direction. These are static, illustrative concepts, not working sign-in or onboarding screens. Copy, show tiles, and layout can be refined after a choice.

What prompted this: desktop sign-in started for 80 of 330 visitors (24%), versus 39% on phones. On the welcome screen, 39 of 78 closed early. The reasons are unknown; the concepts below are hypotheses to test, not established causes.

Desktop sign-in

All three retain Google, Apple, and email, plus the account, email and policy disclosure. The examples use the generic sign-in context; action-specific copy would still be needed.
