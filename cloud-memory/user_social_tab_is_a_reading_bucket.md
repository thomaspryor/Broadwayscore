The owner keeps newsletters filtered to the Gmail **Social** tab on purpose:
"I like my Social emails. They're like a Maybe I'll read bucket. Something to
browse and scroll. That's why they're in that tab." (2026-07-26)

As of 2026-07-26 that tab held 5,395 of the 5,462 messages in the inbox —
Substack, The Stage, Broadway Briefing, Peter Attia, Marginalian. Any session
that measures inbox size will see a number that looks like a cleanup emergency.
It isn't. Several of those senders (The Stage, Broadway Briefing) are trade
press the owner works from.

**Why:** The high count is the feature, not the failure — it's a scroll queue,
not a backlog. Archiving it would destroy something the owner values and can't
easily reconstruct, and unlike alert noise there is no "resolved" test that
makes it safe.

**How to apply:** When the owner says "clean up my email," that means the
automated/bot mail (Forums tab: broadwayscorecard.com alerts, github.com bots)
— archive those after verifying each condition is actually resolved. Leave
Social untouched and don't offer to prune it. If a future ask explicitly names
newsletters, get the spare-list of senders first rather than filtering by age.
Related: [[user_tabs_unread_layman_reporting]] is about cmux tabs, not Gmail
tabs — don't conflate them.
