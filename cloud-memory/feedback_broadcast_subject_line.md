Broadcast name in Resend is an internal label (e.g. "Titanique Opening Night"), NOT the subject line recipients see. The subject line must always follow the established pattern: **"[Show] Critic Reviews Are In — Critic Score: XX"** (e.g. "Titanique Critic Reviews Are In — Critic Score: 76"). This matches the H1 inside the email body.

**Why:** Titanique opening night email (2026-04-13) was sent with the broadcast name "Titanique Opening Night" as the subject instead of the standard pattern. Every previous email had used "[Show] Critic Reviews Are In — Critic Score: XX". Caught too late to fix.

**How to apply:** When reviewing a Resend broadcast draft before send, always check the subject line field independently — confirm it follows the "[Show] Critic Reviews Are In — Critic Score: XX" pattern and is not just the broadcast name. Add this to the pre-send checklist alongside visual review and link checks.
