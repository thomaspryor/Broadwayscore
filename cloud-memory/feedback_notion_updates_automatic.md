Updating Notion cards (outcomes, marking Done, creating new discovery cards) is session discipline, not a suggestion to present to the user. Just do it.

**Why:** User had to tell me to do it after I listed it as a "Do now" item in /what-else. It should have happened automatically throughout the session and at wrap-up.

**How to apply:** After completing work, immediately update the Notion card. After discovering new work, immediately create a new card. Never list Notion updates as optional next steps.
