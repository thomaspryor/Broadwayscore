Reddit post analyzing most-revived Broadway musicals and Shakespeare productions since 1945. Two files committed to main:

- `reddit-revival-post.md` — polished Reddit draft for r/Broadway, ready to post
- `most-revived-broadway-analysis.md` — full reference doc with Off-Broadway data, Encores! pipeline, Shakespeare in the Park comparisons

**Key findings in the post:**
- ~13-year revival cycle for top musicals (Gypsy, Fiddler, King and I all converge)
- No musical premiered after 1964 has reached 5+ Broadway revivals
- Hamlet leads Broadway Shakespeare (13 productions) but hasn't been staged since 2009
- Broadway prefers tragedies; Shakespeare in the Park prefers comedies
