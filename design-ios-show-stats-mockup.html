Screen 1 · Overview

The marquee: parity with Mezzanine in one screen

Everything Mezzanine's stats tab does, compressed into the first scroll — then the signature modules get the room below.

a

Hero tiles. Shows · hours in a seat (Σ runtime, "≈ 20 days" sub-copy) · theaters · this-year with pace vs. last year. Trakt hours · Strava pace

b

Scope pill re-filters every module (All time / 2026 / 2025…). Tapping a year bar does the same. Numbers animate with numericText transitions. Letterboxd year pages, one screen

c

Ratings histogram with a community ghost — dashed outline is everyone's ratings of the same shows, so "you rate +0.3★ above the crowd" is honest. Mezzanine's histogram has no reference line. Letterboxd vs-average · StoryGraph

d

Records strip under the bars: busiest month, current monthly streak, longest drought. Every pill taps to the list behind it. Strava personal records

Screen 2 · Signature

You vs. the Critics — the module Mezzanine can't build

Joins your ★ (×20) against the same critic scores the site publishes. This is the reason to log in Scorecard instead of anywhere else.

a

Taste alignment gauge with a friendly label ("Certified contrarian" under 45%). Spearman ρ in fine print for the nerds.

b

Contrarian picks — largest deltas in both directions, score chips in the site's score-bucket colors, delta badges. The screenshot-bait row, and a one-tap share card.

c

Critical Gold coverage closes a booking loop no tracker has: the unseen remainder is a to-see list deep-linking to show pages and tickets. Stats → intent → ticket.

d

Thresholds: module hides under 5 rated shows with critic scores; diary-only entries (regional/tour catalog) simply don't join, mirroring Mezzanine's "177 of 195 have a location" footnote pattern.

Screen 3 · Signature

The Canon & the People

Letterboxd's two stickiest stats — milestone lists and most-watched people — translated to theater, from data already shipping in mobile-shows.json (tg tags, ct casts/creatives) plus a small pre-computed stats-canon.json.

a

Poster shelves with dimmed unseen entries create the completionist itch. Collections: Best Musical, Best Play, this season's nominees, NYT Critic's Picks, Pulitzers. Letterboxd milestone lists

b

"Saw it before it won" — diary date vs. ceremony date. The purest flex stat in theater; nobody else can compute it.

c

People ranked by appearances across your diary; crossing ×3 auto-creates a micro-collection ("Your Sondheim count: 6"). Letterboxd most-watched directors

d

The mix — musicals/plays donut (chart gold vs. purple, the validated pair), revivals share, and an earliness profile from opening dates: previews / first month / later. StoryGraph donuts

Screen 4 · Parity, then depth

Theaters: keep their map, win at street level

Mezzanine's map and rings are its best feature — we keep both, then use theater-metadata.json (capacity, opened, 43 houses) to go deeper.

a

Theater-district mode: world map clusters by city (parity), but zooming into NYC/London flips to one pin per house — gold visited, outline not. MapKit; needs lat/lng added to theater metadata (small data task in this repo).

b

The house grid is the bingo card people screenshot — every chip tappable to your shows in that house; unvisited chips are the to-do list. Rings tap into it.

c

House records from capacity/opened metadata: home theater, biggest, smallest, oldest. Trivia that makes theater nerds feel seen.

Screen 5 · The December moment

Curtain Call — a year in review worth posting

Eight swipeable full-screen cards, unlocked Nov 15 by push notification, every card exportable as a 9:16 story or 1:1 square via SwiftUI ImageRenderer — no server.

a

Card run: cover collage → the numbers → your year in bars → top five podium → taste card (alignment + most contrarian take) → people card → canon card (Tonys seen, houses unlocked) → sign-off. Spotify Wrapped · Letterboxd YIR

b

Every export carries the wordmark + QR. Wrapped taught everyone: the recap is the acquisition channel. Sharing is always explicit — the diary itself stays private.

c

Share glyphs (⇪) on every stats module reuse the same card renderer year-round: marquee numbers, contrarian picks, the theater bingo grid, any canon shelf.

V1 · offline-computable

Hero tiles, shows over time, ratings histogram, the mix, records, completion rings + house grid, scope pill, tap-through lists. Pure StatsEngine over diary + mobile-shows; Swift Charts.

V1.1 · needs small data artifacts

You vs. Critics/Audience, Tony & Canon (stats-canon.json), People, MapKit district map (theater lat/lng), share cards.

V2 · December

Curtain Call story + push unlock, community ghost overlay, habits heatmap, milestone toasts ("that was show #200").

Ground rules baked in: every number taps to the shows behind it · modules hide below data thresholds instead of rendering thin charts · Mezzanine CSV import is the empty state's call to action · chart marks use the validated gold/purple pair; score-bucket green/amber/red appear only as semantic score chips · single dark theme, on purpose.
