Screen 1 · The priorities, first scroll

Overview: years, theaters, ratings — nothing exotic

Every mark is brand gold on the existing surfaces; identity comes from labels, not extra hues. Layout is the only bold move: the four priority modules fill the first scroll.

a

Hero tiles — shows, hours (Σ runtime), theaters, this-year pace. Tabular numerals, existing card component.

b

Scope pill holds both time systems: seasons first ("2025–26 Season" — theater years end with the Tonys; boundary = ceremony to ceremony), then calendar years, then All time. Current season is the default. Tapping a bar in Shows per year scopes to that year; bars become months.

c

Broadway theaters teaser ring sits on the first scroll and opens the full tracker (screen 2). The "4 to go" line makes it actionable immediately.

d

Ratings histogram with a neutral your-average marker — no second accent color. Footer nudges the 9 unrated entries.

Screen 2 · Priority: theater tracking

Broadway Theaters: the checklist is the feature

Mezzanine gives a ring and a map. We make the 41 houses themselves the object — a scannable checklist backed by theater-metadata.json (capacity, opened year).

a

Every chip taps to a house sheet: your shows there with dates, capacity, opened year — and for unvisited houses, what's playing there now with a ticket link. The completion mechanic feeds actual bookings.

b

Denominator honesty: ring counts currently-operating houses only (41); closed/renamed houses appear as "extra credit" so the 100% is reachable.

c

House records — home theater, biggest, smallest, oldest — trivia straight from existing metadata, no new data needed.

d

Map comes v1.1 (MapKit, one pin per house, filled vs. outlined) once lat/lng lands in theater metadata. The checklist doesn't wait for it.

Screen 3 · Priority: the private feed

Feed: your theater scrapbook, visible to you only

Mezzanine's feed is text. Ours is the visual record of every night at the theater — Playbills, marquees, curtain calls — and it is never public.

a

Log sheet gains "Add photos" (up to 6). iOS-native assist: an on-device photo-library query for shots taken the night of date_seen offers a one-tap "From that night" row. Nothing is scanned server-side.

b

Two views, one dataset: Timeline (month headers, entry cards with photo strips) and Photo wall (edge-to-edge grid of every photo, tapping back to its entry). The poster wall already exists as the profile Grid tab — external review flagged a third in-feed view as redundant, so it's cut. Text-only entries render as clean poster+note cards; the photo nudge is dismissible, never nagware.

c

Privacy is architectural: owner-only storage (private bucket + RLS), EXIF GPS stripped on upload, "🔒 Only you" pinned in the header, and photos never auto-appear in any share or export.

d

Empty-state nudge on photoless entries keeps the scrapbook filling itself forward — and backward, via the "From that night" query on historical entries.

Screen 4 · Signature

You vs. the Critics — the module Mezzanine can't build

Joins your ★ (×20) against the same critic scores the site publishes. Ring and accents are brand gold; the green/amber/red chips are the app's existing semantic score colors, used only as score chips.

a

Taste alignment gauge with a friendly label ("Certified contrarian" under 45%); Spearman ρ in fine print for the nerds.

b

Contrarian picks — largest deltas in both directions. The screenshot-bait rows, and a one-tap share card (v1.1).

c

Critical Gold coverage closes a booking loop no tracker has: the unseen remainder deep-links to show pages and tickets.

d

Aisle Mates — press seats are aisle seats: per-critic alignment (% of ≥5 shared shows within ≈half a star), top 3 + a lovingly-framed Critic Nemesis. Tap a critic → full agreement history + "their picks you haven't seen": your best-matched critic becomes your personal recommender. Corpus supports it — 140 critics have 25+ scored shows. Computed on-device from a compact stats-reviews.json; your ratings never leave the phone.

e

Your Paper of Record — same math per outlet ("the outlet that reads you best"), with a least-aligned counterpart. Works even when the answer isn't the Times.

f

Thresholds: the module hides under 5 rated shows with critic scores; Aisle Mates needs ≥5 shows shared with a single critic (nemesis ≥8); diary-only entries simply don't join, with a footnote count.

Screen 5 · Signature

The Canon & the People

Letterboxd's two stickiest stats — milestone lists and most-watched people — translated to theater, from data already shipping in mobile-shows.json (tg tags, ct casts/creatives) plus a small pre-computed stats-canon.json.

a

Poster shelves with dimmed unseen entries create the completionist itch. Collections: Best Musical, Best Play, this season's nominees, NYT Critic's Picks, Pulitzers.

b

"Saw it before it won" — diary date vs. ceremony date. The purest theater flex; nobody else can compute it.

c

People ranked by appearances across your diary; crossing ×3 auto-creates a micro-collection ("Your Sondheim count: 6").

d

The mix donut stays single-accent: gold + surface-overlay fills with direct labels — no second hue enters the system.

Screen 6 · Two curtain calls a year

Season Finale (post-Tonys) + Year in Review (December)

The theater year ends on Tony night, not December 31 — so the flagship recap is the Season Finale, unlocking the morning after the Tony Awards. The calendar-year edition stays for Wrapped-season culture. One card engine, two editions; eight swipeable cards each, exportable 9:16 / 1:1 via SwiftUI ImageRenderer.

a

Season Finale ("Your 2025–26 season"): the canon card becomes a Tony-night scorecard — nominees/winners you saw, "7 of 10 Best Musical nominees before the ceremony," saw-it-before-it-won additions. Push lands while Tony chatter is still hot — peak share moment.

b

Season boundary: day after one Tony ceremony → day of the next (ceremony dates ship in stats-canon.json; provisional June 30 end for the in-progress season). The scope pill gets the same season entries — "2025–26 Season" next to calendar years, seasons listed first, current season as the post-onboarding default.

c

Year in Review (Nov 15 unlock, data through Dec 31): identical card run scoped to the calendar year, canon card in its general form. Both editions: wordmark + QR on every export, sharing always explicit, feed photos never auto-included.
